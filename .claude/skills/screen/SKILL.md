# /screen — Design a screen

Given a screen name or purpose:

1. Define its **purpose** (one line), **key elements**, and the **critical interaction**.
2. Write a **paste-ready Google Stitch prompt** that follows the global design system in `docs/WIREFRAMES.md` (calm, trustworthy, one warm accent, privacy indicator visible).
3. Add or update the screen's entry in `docs/WIREFRAMES.md` and keep the **build order** current.

Respect the design principles: calm and non-techy, privacy made visible, zero-friction capture, one-tap confirm, honest copy (never imply therapy/medical). For a full flow or several screens at once, dispatch the `designer` agent instead.
