Vault · Meta hackathon

A private helper that remembers.

Think of Vault as a little notebook on your phone, with a helper that can find things later.

Phone-first. Internet is optional.
The big idea

One helper. A memory that stays with you.

Some jobs happen on your phone. If you choose online help, Vault must show what it will send and ask first.

1. Save a note

“My keys are in the blue drawer.” It stays on the phone.

2. Ask for help

Online help is planned. First, Vault must show exactly what leaves.

3. Find a thing

SAM scanning is planned. Vault should point to a real match in your photo.

4. Find it later

The goal: remember the sighting and answer later, even offline.
